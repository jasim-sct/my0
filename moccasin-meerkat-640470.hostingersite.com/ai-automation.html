What kind of chatbot do I get?

Basic is a simple FAQ and lead-capture bot. Standard is an advanced bot trained on your content. Premium is a full AI agent that can take actions across your systems.

What counts as a workflow?

Any repeated process we automate — lead routing, invoice generation, report delivery, onboarding emails, inventory alerts. Basic covers 3, Standard 8, Premium your full business.

Which tools do you connect?

Anything with an API: your CRM, email, Slack, Sheets, Shopify, WhatsApp, Zapier, Make and custom endpoints.
