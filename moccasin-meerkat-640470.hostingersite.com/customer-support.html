FAQ

Customer Support — questions answered

Which platforms do you work in?

Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, HubSpot, Gorgias, Help Scout and Front. Yours, with your macros and your tone document.

What coverage can you provide?

Overflow from 40 hours a month, out-of-hours from 80, or a full desk from 160. 24/5 rotation is standard; weekends on request.

How do agents learn our product?

Two weeks of shadowing plus a knowledge base we build with you. They answer internally before answering customers, and we measure accuracy before going live.

What are your response times?

Under two minutes first response on live chat, under four hours on email. 78% resolved at first contact on a mature account.

How do you handle escalations?

With the full transcript, the customer's history and a summary of what has been tried. Your team should never have to ask the customer to repeat anything.
