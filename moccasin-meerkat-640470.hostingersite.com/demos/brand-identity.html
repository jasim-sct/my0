05 · Usage Rules

Do

✓Maintain clear space equal to the mark height on all sides.
✓Use the mint variant on dark backgrounds only.
✓Keep minimum width at 24px for digital use.

Don't

✕Stretch, skew or rotate the logo under any circumstance.
✕Apply drop shadows, outlines or gradients to the mark.
✕Place the logo on busy imagery without a contrast scrim.
