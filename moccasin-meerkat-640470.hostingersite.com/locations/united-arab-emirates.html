FAQ

Working with us from United Arab Emirates

Do you work with UAE businesses?

Yes — United Arab Emirates is one of our eight core markets. Gulf Standard Time coverage, Arabic and English bilingual delivery, and experience with Noon as well as Amazon.ae.

What time zone do you work in for United Arab Emirates clients?

We build shifts around GST, so your morning is covered. That is written into the contract rather than promised on a call.

Can you invoice in local currency?

Yes. We invoice in USD by default and in GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD or AED on request. Payment by bank transfer, Wise, Payoneer or card.

Do you understand UAE market specifics?

We write in the correct English variant including date formats and spelling, and we have run campaigns, stores and Amazon accounts for UAE brands. Where local knowledge matters — tax, compliance, platform availability — we say what we know and what we do not.

Is there a time zone surcharge?

No. The rate is the rate regardless of which of our eight markets you are in.
