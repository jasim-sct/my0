FAQ

About these builds

Are these real working applications?

Yes. Every one is a complete front-end application, not a screenshot or clickable prototype. You can add products to a cart, complete a validated checkout, move tasks on a kanban board, run a workflow, score a security audit and export CSV files.

Are these client projects?

These are demonstration builds under invented brand names, produced by our team to show the standard of work. Actual client work is covered by NDA, so we show capability rather than breaching confidentiality. Full case studies are available on a call.

Can I get one of these for my business?

Every project here can be rebuilt with your branding, your data and your goals. Prices are stated on each build and range from $1,999 to $2,999 for a full multi-page application.

Do they connect to a real database?

These run in the browser using local storage so you can explore without signing up. A production build connects to a real backend, live payment processing and your existing systems.

How long does a build like this take?

Three to six weeks depending on scope. The 11-page storefront maps to our 30-day timeline; the smaller studios run three weeks. Every build includes revisions, documentation and 30 days of support.
