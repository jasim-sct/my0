FAQ

Video Editing — questions answered

What length can you handle?

Basic covers up to 3 minutes, Standard 10, Premium 20. Feature-length documentary work is quoted separately.

Do you supply the music?

We recommend and licence from Artlist or Epidemic on your account, so the licence stays with you. We never use unlicensed tracks.

What formats do I get?

Whatever you need — 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 1:1 for feed, plus a broadcast-safe master if required.

How fast is turnaround?

Five days for a short edit, ten to eighteen for longer work. Rush turnaround is available at 50% surcharge when we have capacity.

Can you work with our raw footage?

Yes. Send it via Frame.io, Drive or a hard drive. We work with anything from phone footage to RAW cinema files.
