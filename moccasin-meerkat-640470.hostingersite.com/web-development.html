FAQ

Questions, Answered

How much does a website cost?

Basic is $299 for up to 5 template-based pages in 7 days. Standard is $799 for up to 10 custom pages in 14 days. Premium is $1,999 for unlimited pages with fully custom UI/UX and animations in 21-25 days.

Will my site be mobile responsive?

Every tier is responsive. Premium adds scroll animations and micro-interactions like the ones on this site.

Is SEO included?

Basic includes foundational on-page SEO. Standard adds advanced optimization. Premium adds speed and Core Web Vitals tuning.

Can you connect forms to my CRM?

Basic includes 1 form, Standard 3, Premium unlimited forms with CRM integration.

What if I need changes after launch?

Basic includes 2 revision rounds, Standard 4, Premium unlimited within scope. Ongoing care plans are available after handover.
