FAQ

Brand Identity — questions answered

What is in the guidelines document?

Logo usage with clear-space and minimum sizes, the full palette with hex, RGB and CMYK values plus contrast ratios, the type scale, motion timings, application examples and an explicit do-not list.

Do you include motion?

On Premium, yes — we specify entry and exit timings, easing curves and stagger values, because a mark designed without motion in mind animates badly.

How long does a full identity take?

Three weeks for a focused brand, six for something with packaging and multiple sub-brands. We show three flat directions before refining, so you choose early.

Can you rebrand without losing recognition?

Usually the right answer is evolution rather than revolution. We will show you both and be honest about which your audience will actually notice.

Who owns the trademark?

You do. We assign full rights on payment. We do not file trademarks — that needs a lawyer and we will introduce you to one.
