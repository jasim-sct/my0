FAQ

Questions, Answered

How long are the blog articles?

Basic delivers 3 articles at 1,000 words each. Standard delivers 8 articles, Premium 15 — all researched, SEO-optimised and human-written.

Do you write product descriptions?

Yes — 10 on Basic, 30 on Standard, 60 on Premium, written for both search engines and buyers.

What video work do you handle?

Basic covers editing up to 3 minutes. Standard adds professional effects up to 10 minutes. Premium delivers full motion graphics up to 20 minutes.

Is AI writing the content?

No. We use AI for research and outlining, then humans write and edit. Pure AI content does not rank and does not sell.
