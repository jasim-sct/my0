FAQ

Questions, Answered

How many keywords do you target?

SEO Basic covers 5 keywords, Standard 15, Premium 30 — chosen with you based on search volume and buying intent.

How many backlinks are included?

10 on Basic, 30 on Standard, 70 on Premium. All manually placed on relevant sites — never spam directories.

How long before SEO works?

Expect early movement in 4 to 8 weeks. Competitive keywords typically take 4 to 6 months of consistent work.

Is ad spend included in the ads price?

No. Management fees are separate from your ad budget. Basic manages up to $1,000 spend, Standard up to $3,000, Premium unlimited.

What do social media packages include?

12 to 60 posts a month, 8 to 40 stories or reels, across 2 to 5 platforms. Standard and Premium add community engagement, and Premium adds ad management.

How often do I get reports?

Monthly on Basic, bi-weekly on Standard, and weekly plus a strategy call on Premium.
