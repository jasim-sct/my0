FAQ

Pricing questions

Why publish prices at all?

Because withholding them wastes everyone's time. You should know whether you can afford us before booking a call, and we should not spend an hour discovering you cannot.

Are these fixed prices?

The starting figures are real. Final quotes are fixed before work begins — we do not bill hourly for delivery work because it rewards being slow.

Can I mix services?

Yes. Take three or more and it becomes a retainer at a lower blended rate. Most clients start with one service and expand once the reporting earns trust.

What payment terms?

One-off projects are 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Retainers bill monthly in advance. Bank transfer, Wise, Payoneer and card all accepted.

Is there a contract?

Monthly retainers cancel with 30 days written notice. No exit fee, no minimum term beyond the first month, and full handover documentation either way.

Do you offer discounts?

Fifty percent off any service for registered non-profits, permanently. Volume discounts apply automatically above three services.
