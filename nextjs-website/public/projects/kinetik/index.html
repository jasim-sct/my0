The problem we solve

Design that
stops at the
static file.

Most studios hand over a logo and a PDF. Then a separate motion team tries to animate something that was never built to move, and a separate editor grades footage that ignores the palette entirely.

We do all four in-house. The identity is designed knowing how it will animate. The film is graded to the brand colours. Nothing gets translated twice.
