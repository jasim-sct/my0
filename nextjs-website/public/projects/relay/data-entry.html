What we handle

Types of work

Web scraping

Directories, marketplaces, listings and public registries. We respect robots.txt and rate limits — no scraped site has ever blocked a client.

  • Structured export to CSV, Sheets or direct to CRM
  • Scheduled re-runs to keep data fresh
  • Change detection between runs

Data entry & cleaning

Manual entry where automation cannot reach, plus deduplication, normalisation and enrichment of what you already hold.

  • CRM cleanup and merge
  • Format normalisation — phone, address, name casing
  • Two-pass verification on every batch
Quality

How accuracy is enforced

99.4% target

Measured on a random 5% sample of every batch, checked by someone who did not enter it.

Two-pass entry

Critical fields are entered twice by different people. Mismatches are flagged rather than guessed.

Free re-runs

If a batch falls below target, we redo it at our cost. That has happened three times in four years.
