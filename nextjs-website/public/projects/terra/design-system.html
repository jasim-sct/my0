The Terra design system

The tokens, components and rules the whole brand is built from. This is the document we hand over at the end of a UI/UX engagement.

01 · Colour

Earth tones with one warm accent. Every pairing below meets WCAG AA for body text.

03 · Buttons & inputs

Buttons

Primary is forest green and turns clay on hover — the one deliberate colour shift in the system. Radius is always fully rounded. Every button lifts 2px on hover.

Focus rings use sage at 18% opacity. Errors use clay, never pure red.

05 · Spacing & radius

Scale

A 4px base. Sections breathe on a fluid clamp between 52 and 104 pixels.

Corner radius

12 — inputs

full — buttons

Organic shapes carry the brand: the hero image uses an arch, section joins use a hand-drawn wave rather than a straight edge.

06 · Principles

Four rules we held to

Warmth over contrast

Nothing sits on pure white or pure black. Cream and ink keep the page calm on a screen at 6am.

Photography does the selling

The interface stays quiet so the produce can be the loudest thing on the page.

Curves, not corners

Arches, waves and full-round buttons. Nothing about a farm is a rectangle.

Plain words

"Picked this morning" beats "farm-fresh quality produce" every time.
